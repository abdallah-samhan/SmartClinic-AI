# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or socioeconomic status.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- **Being respectful**: Treat all community members with respect and dignity
- **Being inclusive**: Welcome people of all backgrounds and experience levels
- **Being constructive**: Provide helpful, constructive feedback
- **Being collaborative**: Work together and share knowledge
- **Being honest**: Be truthful and transparent in all communications
- **Being patient**: Understand that people have different perspectives and expertise

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or violence (verbal or otherwise)
- Sexist, racist, or otherwise derogatory comments
- Personal attacks or insults
- Publishing private information without consent
- Deliberate intimidation or threats
- Trolling, spamming, or disruptive behavior
- Exclusionary jokes or language

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All community spaces (GitHub, discussions, issues, pull requests)
- Official community events
- Unofficial events representing the community
- Any communication representing the project or community

## Enforcement

Violations of this code may result in:

1. **Warning**: First-time minor violations
2. **Temporary Ban**: Repeated or serious violations (1-30 days)
3. **Permanent Ban**: Severe or repeated violations

### Reporting

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it:

- **Email**: conduct@smartclinic.ai
- **GitHub**: Report through GitHub's abuse report feature
- **Private Message**: Contact a maintainer directly

All reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy of the reporter.

## Consequences

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other members of project leadership.
